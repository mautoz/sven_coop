    Player Commands 🇺🇸

    Insurgency Weapons — Weapon shop

    Opens the weapon buy menu using money earned during the map.

    /buy

    Money is earned by score. Starting balance: 5000.

    RockTheVote — Change map

    Command (in chat) Description
    rtv Vote to change the current map
    nominate <map> Nominate a map for the vote

    Wait 30 seconds after joining before you can vote.

    Lt-Parachute — Parachute

    • Open: hold E (use) while falling
    • Close: release E

    CLevels — XP and Level System

    Earn XP by completing maps and killing enemies. Spend skill points to upgrade your character.

    Command (in chat) Description
    /menu Main menu
    /selectskills Choose your skills
    /resetskills Reset skills to reallocate points
    /skillsinfo Details on all skills
    /selectspecials Choose special skills (requires medals)
    /resetspecials Reset special skills
    /specialsinfo Details on all special skills
    /handicaps Enable handicaps for bonus XP
    /handicapsinfo Details on all handicaps
    /character Your character summary
    /xpgain XP earned on the current map
    /achievements Achievements menu

    Skills

    # Skill Effect
    1 Strength Higher starting HP
    2 Superior Armor Higher starting armor
    3 Regeneration Regenerates HP over time
    4 Nano Armor Regenerates armor over time
    5 Ammo Reincarnation Automatically restores ammo
    6 Anti-Gravity Device Reduces gravity — jump higher (hold Jump)
    7 Awareness Slightly improves several other skills
    8 Team Power Supports nearby teammates with HP and AP
    9 Block Attack Chance to fully block incoming attacks
    10 Medals Improves all skills + XP bonus; required for Special Skills

    Special Skills

    Only available with medals (1 medal = 1 special point).

    # Special Skill Effect
    1 Starting Attack On revive/respawn, deals damage to nearby enemies
    2 Portable Dispencer Doubles all regenerated ammo
    3 Ubercharge Increases the max capacity of all basic skills
    4 Quick Heal Extra medkit ammo every few seconds
    5 Demoman Chance to gain M16 grenades
    6 Practice Shot Chance to fire without consuming ammo
    7 BioElectric Increases Shock Rifle max capacity
    8 Medical Emergency Heal allies at range with the Medkit (tertiary attack)
    9 Red Cross Increases Medkit max capacity

    Handicaps

    Make the game harder in exchange for bonus XP. Can only be activated at map start.

    # Handicap Effect XP Bonus
    1 Medical Phobia Cannot be healed (except by regeneration) +22%
    2 Obsolete Technology Cannot repair armor (except Nano Armor) +27%
    3 Nitrogen Blood Cannot be revived; always gibs on death +13%
    4 Karmic Retribution All damage received also poisons you +18%
    5 Realism Hides the HUD (suit) +22%
    6 Big Explosion Explosions deal much more damage +18%
    7 Limited Equipment Restricted to only one weapon +18%
    8 Dead Weight Increased gravity — harder to jump +18%
    9 Lacking Help Partially disables Medals and Special Skills +13%
    10 Dirty Mag Ammo left in the magazine is lost on reload +22%
    11 Lost Bullets Restricted to only one clip per weapon +18%
    12 Weak Restart On revive/respawn, starts with 25 HP and 0 AP +31%
    13 Dangerous Waters Instant drowning underwater +13%
    14 Bleeding View Concussion effect that worsens as HP drops +18%
    15 Health Crisis Medkit capacity cut in half +15%
